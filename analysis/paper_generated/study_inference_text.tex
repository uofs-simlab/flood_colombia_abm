The largest setting-specific 95\% Monte Carlo half-width is 0.508 percentage points; 2 of 24 settings exceed the prespecified 0.5-percentage-point flag. This precision assessment measures variability among random seeds under fixed assumptions. It does not measure uncertainty in the assumptions or real-world outcomes. Observing zero deaths in every sampled run would not establish zero risk.
