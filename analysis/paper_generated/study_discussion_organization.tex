The mean paired mortality change from no to full organization is -7.85 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval -8.31 to -7.40). Depth-only and social-only mortality medians are 0.21\% and 7.97\%, compared with 0.15\% under both channels. The depth-only median is closer to the combined-channel median than the social-only median. This suggests that the assumed depth reduction drives most of the modeled improvement, but similar medians do not prove that depth-only and combined protection are equivalent. The social-only paired interval spans zero: the sampled runs do not clearly resolve whether this change lowers or raises mortality, and they do not prove that its effect is absent. The depth reduction is imposed by the researcher rather than produced by simulated community action. These comparisons separate its consequences from those of the social-capital scores; they do not rank the real-world value of community interventions.
