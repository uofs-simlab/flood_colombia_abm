The mean paired mortality change from no to full organization is -7.85 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval -8.31 to -7.40). Depth-only and social-only mortality medians are 0.21\% and 7.97\%, compared with 0.15\% under both channels. These results suggest that the prescribed depth reduction accounts for most of the modeled change. The social-only paired interval spans zero, leaving its direction unresolved. The channel comparison isolates consequences of the model's depth and social-score pathways.
