We simulate a population of 972 people over 408 hours, using 480 runs across 24 settings and 20 random seeds per setting. Community organization is represented by two separate assumptions: reduced flood depth and increased social-capital scores. Rescue experiments vary shelter capacity, location and boat fleet size. Median mortality is 7.72\% without organization and 0.15\% with full organization, compared with 0.21\% when only depth is reduced and 7.97\% when only social-capital scores increase. The mean paired change from no to full organization is -7.85 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval -8.31 to -7.40). With shelter places for 30\% of the population, median mortality is 4.12\% at the nearer site and 4.99\% at the farther site. At the farther site with 10\% shelter capacity, the medians are 6.64\% with one boat and 6.48\% with five boats.
