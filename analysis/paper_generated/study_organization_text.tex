At organization levels 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1, median mortality at hour 408 is 7.72\%, 5.76\%, 3.70\%, 1.75\%, 0.15\%, respectively (Table~\ref{tab:organization}). Figure~\ref{fig:organization} connects results sharing the same seed and shows mortality alongside the largest hourly proportion stranded. The mean seed-paired change from no to full organization is -7.85 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval -8.31 to -7.40).

The depth-only and social-only medians are 0.21\% and 7.97\%, respectively. With weaker depth attenuation, the median is 6.17\%. The channel-separated results (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}; Figure~\ref{fig:ablation}) separate the effect of reducing sampled flood depths from the effect of increasing social-capital scores in the decision rules. Figure~\ref{fig:effects} displays the mean paired differences and their Monte Carlo intervals. An interval spanning zero is compatible with differences in either direction; it does not prove that the settings are equivalent.
