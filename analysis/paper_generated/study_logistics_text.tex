Figure~\ref{fig:logistics} and Tables~\ref{tab:distance}--\ref{tab:fleet} summarize the capacity--site and fleet designs. With shelter places for 30\% of the population, median mortality is 4.12\% at the near site and 4.99\% at the far site. The mean paired change from the near to the far site is +1.10 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval +0.81 to +1.39). Distance comparisons are tested separately at each shelter capacity. A significant result at one capacity and a nonsignificant result at another do not, by themselves, show that capacity changes the distance effect.

At the far site with 10\% shelter capacity, median mortality is 6.64\% with one boat and 6.48\% with five boats. The mean paired one-to-five-boat change is -0.24 percentage points (95\% Monte Carlo interval -0.46 to -0.03), with within-family Holm-adjusted $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.092468$. Its absolute mean change is smaller than the full-organization difference. However, the experiments use different resources and reference conditions; without comparable costs they cannot establish which option gives better value for money.
